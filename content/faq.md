        {{< faq-item text="What should I wear?">}}
        The dress code is semi-formal/dressy casual.
        
        Folks who'd like to wear a dress can wear sundresses or lightweight separates. Alternatively, guests can wear a light dress shirt with khakis or slacks. Jackets and ties are optional. Florals and/or bright colors are optional, but encouraged!
        
        We expect the temperature to be warm and mild, but weather can be unpredictable in Colorado. Feel free to bring extra layers in case it is chilly. We suggest flat shoes or wedge heels for the outdoor ceremony on the grass.
        {{< /faq-item >}}

        {{< faq-item text="Do you have a room block?" >}}
        No. Chautauqua lodging does not operate like a normal hotel and does not have the option for a room block.
        {{< /faq-item >}}

        {{< faq-item text="When is the RSVP deadline?" >}}
        Please RSVP by [TBD].
        {{< /faq-item >}}

        {{< faq-item text="Where is the ceremony?" >}}
        The Enchanted Mesa Garden is a beautiful outdoor garden space at Chautauqua. It is located across the street from the Academic Hall, next to the picnic shelter.
        {{< /faq-item >}}

        {{< faq-item text="Where is the reception?" >}}
        The Community House is a short 2 minute walk from the Enchanted Mesa Garden. We will hold cocktail hour in the basement rooms, dinner and dancing will be held in the Grand Assembly Hall on the main floor. 
        {{< /faq-item >}}

        {{< faq-item text="Where do I park?" >}}
        Parking is free, but limited. You can park in the Academic Hall parking lot, the dirt lot next to the picnic shelter, or any available street parking in Chautauqua. [Include map]
        {{< /faq-item >}}

        {{< faq-item text="Is everything wheelchair accessible?" >}}
        There are two small steps to get up to the ceremony location. The Community House basement is accessible from the outside, and the Grand Assembly Hall is accessible via a lift outside the main steps. If you need additional accommodations or information, please let us know.
        {{< /faq-item >}}

        {{< faq-item text="Can I bring so-and-so?" >}}
        We are so happy that more people want to celebrate with us, but the venue is small and has a strict capacity. Please keep your party to those listed on the invitation.
        {{< /faq-item >}}
